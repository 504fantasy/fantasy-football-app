How This Game Works

Every week (Weeks 1–{{ total_weeks }}) you pick players at each position: QB · RB · WR · TE · DST · K.
The number of players per position is set by your commissioner in the league settings.
Once you use a player, they're locked out for you for the rest of the season.
There's no draft — just smart weekly selection. The team with the most fantasy points at the end of Week {{ total_weeks }} wins.
